The real estate industry will bury a new agent in advice. Your broker will tell you one thing. The top producer in your office will tell you another. The training program will suggest a third. The internet will offer fifty-seven more.

Most of it is not wrong. Almost none of it is useful when you're trying to figure out what to do on Monday morning.

This playbook cuts through that. One plan. Ninety days. The activities that actually create a pipeline when you're starting from nothing.

## The 90-Day Foundation: What You're Actually Building

The goal of your first 90 days is not to close a transaction — though you might. The goal is to build three things:

1. **A prospecting habit** — a daily activity that you do consistently, at a set time, without negotiating with yourself about it
2. **A contact database** — a real list of real people with real contact information who know you're in real estate
3. **A follow-up system** — a way to stay in front of people without manually remembering everyone

Everything else can wait. Branding, social media, a website, a farming strategy, an online lead program — all of it is secondary to building these three foundations.

:::callout
**Why This Order Matters:** Most new agents spend their first 90 days on activities that feel productive but don't create pipeline. Setting up their CRM perfectly. Designing business cards. Building their Instagram presence. None of these activities produce conversations with potential clients. Prospecting does. Build the prospecting habit first.
:::

## Days 1–30: Get Into Action

### Your Database Is Everyone You Know

Start a spreadsheet. Add every person you know — family, friends, former colleagues, neighbors, people you went to school with, people from your gym, your church, your kid's school. Add anyone you'd say hello to if you passed them in a grocery store.

Don't pre-screen this list. Don't assume anyone is or isn't going to buy or sell a home. Just add them. The goal is 200+ contacts in your first week.

For each contact: first name, last name, phone, email, how you know them. That's it.

### The Sphere Activation Call

Once your list is built, call every person on it. Not to sell them anything — to tell them what you're doing.

> "Hey, it's [Name]. I just wanted to let you know I got my real estate license and I'm working with [Brokerage]. If you ever need anything — or if you know someone buying or selling — I'd love the chance to help. I'm not calling to pitch you, just wanted to make sure you knew what I was up to."

Most people are supportive. Some will refer you immediately. A few will be in the market themselves. All of them now know you're in real estate.

This call is the most important prospecting activity you will do in your first 30 days. Make every call.

## Days 31–60: Establish Your Lead Generation Habit

By day 31, your sphere knows what you do. Now you need a consistent source of new conversations.

Pick one lead generation activity and commit to it for 60 days:
- Cold calling (geographic farm)
- Door knocking (geographic farm)
- FSBO/Expired prospecting

:::warning
**The Single Activity Rule:** Don't try to do all three at once. Agents who split their time between cold calling, door knocking, and FSBO prospecting simultaneously get mediocre results at all three. Pick one. Get good at it. Add a second activity at the 90-day mark.
:::

Whatever you pick, schedule it at a fixed time every day. 9am to 11am. 4pm to 6pm. Whatever fits your life. The time block is non-negotiable — it is the equivalent of punching a clock.

## Days 61–90: Build the Follow-Up System

By now you have prospecting contacts piling up from two months of daily outreach. The pipeline compounds when you follow up consistently. Without a follow-up system, every contact you made in the first 60 days is just wasted effort.

A basic follow-up system has three stages:

| Stage | Timeline | Method | Message |
|-------|----------|--------|---------|
| Hot | 1–7 days | Phone + text | Reference your specific conversation |
| Warm | 7–30 days | Phone | Market update or relevant news |
| Nurture | 30+ days | Phone every 60 days | Value-first, no ask |

Put every prospecting contact into one of these three buckets in your CRM. Set follow-up reminders. Work the system every day alongside your new prospecting.

## What Success Looks Like at Day 90

By day 90, you should have:
- 300+ contacts in your database
- A daily prospecting habit (30–90 minutes, consistently) 
- At least 2–3 listing appointments on your calendar or completed
- A CRM with follow-up cadences running for your top contacts
- At least one transaction in progress or closed

If you have those five things, your first 90 days were successful — regardless of your GCI. The foundation is built. Now you build on it.

## FAQ

**What if I don't know 200 people?**
You know more people than you think. Go through your phone contacts. Go through your social media followers. Go through old emails. You'll be surprised. If you genuinely only have 100 contacts, start there — but also start geographic prospecting immediately to build your database faster.

**Should I pick a geographic farm or sphere of influence as my focus?**
Both. Your sphere is your warmest possible source of referrals. Your farm is your long-term pipeline. Activate your sphere in days 1–30. Start building your farm in days 31+.

**How many hours should I be working per day?**
There is no minimum. There is a minimum on prospecting activity: 60–90 minutes of active prospecting per day, every business day. Outside of that, your time is yours to fill with whatever supports the business.

**When should I start building my brand/social media?**
After 90 days. Not because branding doesn't matter — it does. But it doesn't matter as much as the prospecting habit in your first 90 days. Build the pipeline first. Build the brand on top of it.
