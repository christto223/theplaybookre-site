Most agents run open houses as a favor to their seller. Set out some cookies, hand out a flyer, answer questions for three hours, and go home with a sign-in sheet full of names they'll never call.

That's not an open house strategy. That's a Sunday shift.

A properly executed open house is one of the most efficient lead generation tools available to a real estate agent — especially early in your career when your marketing budget is limited but your time is not.

This playbook covers the complete system: how to prepare before the first visitor walks in, how to work the room while they're there, and how to follow up in a way that actually converts.
