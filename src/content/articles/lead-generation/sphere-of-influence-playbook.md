Your sphere of influence is the most underutilized asset in your business. You already have the relationships. You just don't have a system to activate them.

Most agents treat their SOI like a passive list. They send a market update twice a year, show up at a holiday party, and hope that when someone decides to buy or sell, they'll remember to call.

That's not a strategy. That's luck.

This playbook is about converting your existing relationships into a systematic referral engine.
